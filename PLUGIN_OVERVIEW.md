## Will this window last?

Each bar in bb's usage card gets a tick at even pace: where the bar would be at an exact even rate. Hover a row to see how far ahead or under pace you are, when the quota runs out, and how long you will be without quota before the reset.

## An optional strip

Turn on the footer strip in settings for a chip per provider, for example `44% 1.6× · 2d 15h short`, and a dialog with every window.

## What the numbers mean

The pace is a straight-line projection from the start of the window. Nights and weekends usually lower the real rate. Only providers and hosts that report quota information appear. A fork of Usage Bar by Dmitrii Kapustin.
